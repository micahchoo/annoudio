Recordings

Nineteen short screen recordings, one for each workflow and role. They were made on 2026-10-09 on the real demo stack, with Playwright. A red dot shows the pointer. The videos have no sound. Under each video, the text tells what happens and the stumbles that the recording found.

How they were made, and how to make them again: design/recordings/README.md.

Operator

A person of the team. Works in the admin, on a laptop.

Member

A person of the community. Tells stories on the Annotate page, on a phone.

Visitor

Anyone on the site. Plays the recording and reads the stories in the widget.

Site maintainer

Puts the widget on a site: a plugin, or the snippet by hand.
